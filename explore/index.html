Explore

Adventures from the shore.

The Reef, Off Our Beach

A house reef, captured in a single afternoon.

Every photo below was taken by a diver wading in from our shore — no boat, no long swim. Sea fans, barrel sponges, feather stars, table corals: this is what lives a few fin-kicks from your towel.

Photographed by a guest diver, Binucot Cove. Bring fins — or borrow ours.

Reef Snorkel

Half day

Step in from the cove and meet parrotfish, anemone gardens, and the resident sea turtle. Gear included.

Garing Falls Hike

Half day

A short jungle trail leads to a tiered waterfall and a swim hole cool enough to forget the sun.

Island Hopping

Full day

Charter a banca to Cobrador, Logbon, and Alad — the marble islets of Romblon — for a picnic on empty beaches.

Sunset Sail

Two hours

Glide along the western shore with a glass of local rum as Tablas turns gold then violet.

Shopping & Local Exploration

Island commerce at your fingertips.

Whether you're looking for local treasures or modern conveniences, the best of Tablas Island is just a short, scenic ride away.

Local Charm & Modern Malls

From small village shops to larger stores and upcoming shopping malls, everything you need is within reach.

The Tricycle Experience

Embrace a true Asian attraction. Most local shops and malls are easily accessible by tricycle — a fun and authentic way to see the island.

Worry-Free Travel

As our guest, we are happy to arrange all your transportation, ensuring you can explore the area with total ease and comfort.

Getting here

One short flight from Manila, then a slow road through coconut groves.

Fly into Tugdan Airport (TBH) on Tablas Island. We'll meet you with a private transfer and have a cold buko waiting at the gate.
