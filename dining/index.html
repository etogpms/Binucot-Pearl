Dining

The Cove Kitchen.

A World of Flavor in Every Bite

Led by our owner, plated by our chef.

Our kitchen is led by our owner and head chef, who brings a lifetime of global travel and culinary discovery to your table. We don't believe in mass production — only in the art of the meal.

Global Inspiration

A menu inspired by flavors acquired from around the world, crafted with local ingredients.

Quality Over Quantity

Our motto is simple: every dish must meet the highest standards of flavor and quality. We prioritize excellence in every serving.

Intimate Dining

A cozy, high-standard dining environment designed to complement your relaxing stay.

A small, daily-changing menu.

Our chef writes the menu each morning around what came in on the boats and what's ripe in the garden. Long tables, bare feet, a bottle of Filipino rum — the way dinner should feel by the sea.

To Begin

  • Kinilaw of yellowfin, calamansi, ginger
  • Heart-of-palm salad, toasted coconut

From the Sea

  • Whole grilled lapu-lapu, lemongrass butter
  • Adobong pusit, garlic rice
  • Tinolang manok, native chili

From the Garden

  • Charred eggplant, miso, calamansi
  • Pinakbet, crispy shallots

To End

  • Mango with sticky rice, salted coconut
  • Halo-halo of the day
