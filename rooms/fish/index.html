The Story

A spirit of strength, by the sea.

The Fish is the silent purveyor of Tablas — the morning catch that fed the island, the silver glint under the banca at dawn. The Fish Room owes its name to this gentle abundance.

Soft cream walls, hand-finished hardwoods, and brushed-metal sconces give the room a warm, underwater silence at golden hour. Sliding glass doors open onto a sheltered terrace, where the sea breeze never quite subsides.

The comfortable bathroom — with a rain shower and crystal lamps above the sink — is a small, daily ceremony. Locally-crafted acacia furniture and turquoise accents evoke a sense of tranquility and a maritime aura.
