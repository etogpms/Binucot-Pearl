The Destination

Discover Binucot Beach: a hidden sanctuary.

While others flock to the crowded shores, find peace on the pristine sands of "Little Boracay." Binucot Pearl is the only boutique resort located directly on the beach, offering a front-row seat to the most dazzling sunsets in the Romblon region.

Dive into nature

Step off the sand and immerse yourself in a sanctuary of coral reefs, tropical fish, and sea turtles.

Authentic atmosphere

Immerse yourself in local villages to experience a true Asian welcome and the hospitality of the local people.

Modern comfort

Enjoy a peaceful life without isolation — local towns and shopping centers are within a 30-minute drive.
